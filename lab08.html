The seven-step method

    Five ways to narrow the search

    Bottom-upStart at Layer 1 and climb. Best when links are down or nothing works at all.
    Top-downStart at the application and descend. Best when one app fails but others work.
    Divide and conquerStart at Layer 3 with a ping, then go up or down based on the result.
    Follow the pathTrace the packet hop by hop with tracert and device tables.
    Spot the differenceCompare the broken device to a working one with the same role.

      Look for the pattern before the cause. Symptoms that hit a group of ports at once point to something those ports share: a power budget, an ASIC, a CPU. A problem that grows over weeks and resets with a reboot points to software, not parts.

      Match the tool to the medium: copper gets a certifier with TDR, fiber gets an optical power meter and a scope, Wi-Fi gets a spectrum analyzer. A toner finds cables; it doesn't test them. Loss is Tx minus Rx. SNR is signal minus noise, both in dBm.
