LAN121 Network Essentials, Activity 7

Threat hunt

You are the on-call analyst for Ridgeline Credit Union. Ten incident tickets are waiting. Read the evidence, name the attack behind each one, then build the defenses that would have stopped it.

Field guide: what you need to know (Network+ N10-009, 4.2 and 4.3)

Know the attack

  • Which layer or system it abuses: switch tables, VLAN tags, ARP, DNS, DHCP, Wi-Fi, people, or endpoints.
  • What it threatens: confidentiality (eavesdropping), integrity (redirection), or availability (outage).
  • The evidence it leaves: log messages, address mismatches, traffic patterns.

Read the evidence

  • Interpret arp -a, ipconfig /all, nslookup, and switch output.
  • Compare what you see with what you expect: the right server, gateway, MAC, or SSID.
  • Count things: sources, MAC addresses, badges, people.

Pick the defense

  • Switch hardening: port security, DHCP snooping, DAI, disabling DTP, native VLAN changes.
  • NAC and identity: 802.1X, MFA, WPA3-Enterprise.
  • Layered controls: filtering, IPS, segmentation, backups, training, and physical security.
